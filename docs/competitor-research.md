# Kousa competitor research

Checked September 18, 2026 against first-party product pages and documentation. Scope is Kousa's collaborative canvas for connected AI text, image, video, and speech workflows, as documented in the [README](../README.md). The overlap assessments below are judgments based on documented capabilities, not market-share rankings or hands-on product tests.

| Competitor | Verified capabilities | Competitive relevance, inferred |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Figma Weave**, formerly Weavy | Figma acquired Weavy and renamed it Figma Weave. It combines AI models, professional editing, a node canvas, and reusable workflows. Its current product page includes image, video, audio, and 3D models, plus masking, compositing, and color grading. [Acquisition](https://www.figma.com/blog/welcome-weavy-to-figma/), [product](https://www.figma.com/solutions/figma-ai-tool-weave/) | One of Kousa's closest direct competitors. Overlap spans the canvas, model orchestration, and repeatable creative production; professional editing and Figma distribution make it a priority to study. |
| **FLORA** | A creative-team workflow product with real-time collaboration. Its canvas connects text, image, and video blocks across multiple models. [Quickstart](https://docs.flora.ai/getting-started/quickstart), [canvas](https://docs.flora.ai/editor/canvas) | A close direct competitor for teams building and reusing visual creative processes. Similar product concept and audience. |
| **Magnific Spaces**, formerly Freepik Spaces | Freepik is now Magnific. Spaces has connected text, image, video, and audio nodes; voiceovers; video/audio mixing; node, workflow, and downstream execution; generation history; and credit information. It supports shared canvas editing and comments. [Name change](https://www.magnific.com/freepik), [nodes and execution](https://www.magnific.com/ai/docs/nodes-and-connections), [collaboration](https://www.magnific.com/ai/docs/collaboration-and-sharing) | A close direct competitor with especially broad overlap, including Kousa's speech, selective reruns, and audiovisual output. |
| **Krea Nodes** | Reusable and shareable workflows chain image/video tools, run models in parallel, and automate repetitive work. Prompt-to-Workflow builds an editable node graph from a written request. [Nodes](https://www.krea.ai/features/nodes), [Prompt-to-Workflow](https://www.krea.ai/blog/prompt-to-workflow) | A direct competitor for creators who want model choice and repeatable generation. Natural-language workflow setup is a relevant benchmark for onboarding. |
| **Higgsfield Canvas** | Connected workflows combine multiple models with image/video generation, reusable templates, and live collaboration. [Canvas](https://higgsfield.ai/canvas-intro), [help](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/tools/how-do-i-use-canvas) | A direct competitor, particularly if Kousa targets video creators and small production teams. |
| **Runway Workflows** | Connected text, image, audio, and video nodes; LLMs; templates; branching; locks that preserve selected outputs; video stitching and audio utilities; workspace and link sharing. [Introduction to Workflows](https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/45763528999699-Introduction-to-Workflows) | A direct competitor for repeatable video production. Its workflow product makes Runway relevant beyond single-model video generation. |

Two alternatives also belong on the watchlist.

- **ComfyUI / Comfy Cloud** supports node workflows for image, video, audio, and 3D generation. The open-source core runs locally; Cloud runs workflows on managed GPUs, and paid Partner Nodes provide access to third-party models. This is a direct functional substitute for technically comfortable users, although Kousa's hosted collaboration experience is a different buying proposition. [Official overview](https://support.comfy.org/articles/9806306061-about-comfy)
- **Adobe Firefly Boards** provides a collaborative infinite canvas for images and video, Adobe and partner models, and Creative Cloud integration. It is an adjacent alternative for ideation and creative review. [Official product page](https://www.adobe.com/products/firefly/features/boards.html)

My assessment: start customer comparisons with Figma Weave, FLORA, and Magnific Spaces, then add Krea Nodes, Higgsfield Canvas, and Runway Workflows according to the user's main workflow. A generic "AI tools on a canvas" position already has substantial competition. Voiceover, reusable workflows, and partial workflow execution also have documented competitors. This research does not establish whether they match Kousa's automatic change detection and reuse rules. A stronger position needs a specific customer and production outcome, supported by evidence that Kousa completes that job better.
